03 / MONETISATION

Free. One-off. Or a plan.

Three levers, one entitlement system. Free assets stay free forever; OSS-seeded items live in the free tier; original creator work is paid; plans bundle credits and higher agent quotas.

Agents read for free. Unauthenticated and free-key agents get search, metadata, licence checks, watermarked previews and install snippets — no paid-file downloads. Paid assets require an entitlement; free assets can be fetched by agents with a signed URL.
